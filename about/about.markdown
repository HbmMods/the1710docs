# /Why does this exist
Documentation for Minecraft Forge on 1.7.10 is basically nonexistant. Official docs only existed briefly, covered maybe three topics, and the rest was mediocre tutorials covering maybe a few basics form the year 2015.

This site aims to document how things are made in a format that is hopefully understandable for beginners. When hearing "documentation", most people's response is immediately just writing down every detail in the driest form possible, while not really thinking about who might be reading this and why. This is fine if you only want to look at something for reference, but for beginners it's next to worthless. This page will attempt to do a different approach.

## A lot of documentation is just quite frankly shit
"RTFM" is something that is often thrown around by annoying people who enjoy fellating themselves over their perceived smarts. Sure, read the manual, is the manual any good? No? Well sucks to be you. Doesn't actually help anyone, but at least I can feel good about myself.

A lot of manuals feel like they were written exclusively for people who already know everything about a subject. Which honestly just defeats the point. If i need to read the manual in order to understand the manual, how am I expected to understand the manual to read it?

## Did you consider - ?
In order to make this whole process more effective, it's important to make explanations less robotic and more human, almost like I'm here in person and explaining things instead of writing a manual. Concepts shouuld be properly established first before we can mention them while explaining other things, because there's no point in telling anyone that the glorpulizer is a type of bleemorp when no one knows what the fuck a bleemorp even is. Hence why all pages should list "dependencies" - things you gotta read before you can hope to understand the contents of that page.

Even if I do fail and the docs are incomplete to the point of being useless or just really badly written, at least I made some effort, dammit.
