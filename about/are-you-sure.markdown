# /Are you even sure you want to do this

Modding isn't something you learn in half an afternoon. It's gonna take time, some practice, and a hell of a load of patience to do anything meaningful.

If you see yourself as an ideas guy who just wants to quickly see stuff appear in the game as soon as you come up with something, I have bad news for you. You probably aren't gonna last long enough for that.

If you think you can get ClausGPT to crap out your mod for you, why are you even here? What would you even need documentation for?

However, if you find joy in just making shit for the sake of making it, if you have the patience for getting the hang of things, then you are qualified. Congratulations, welcome to the rest of the page!
