# /Java knowledge

This guide does not include an introduction to Java. Sorry!

This is something you have to bring yourself before you can do any modding. Knowing at least the basics (and ideally well enough that you don't constantly mess up simple syntax, wondering why it won't work) is mandatory. Can't skip that part. I know you want to skip that part. But you can't.

The more you know the better, however grasping the basics and knowing your way around some of the standard libary data types should be enough. Ideally you should know what a HashMap does. Lambdas are kinda cool too, you might want to figure out how functional interfaces work.
